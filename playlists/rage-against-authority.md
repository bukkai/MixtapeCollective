## Sequencing (Nov 2026 rework)

Goal: stay angry. The old order went RATM straight into melodic punk. Now it opens with metal and industrial, gets to punk via crust and d-beat, and stays furious all the way. Current order lives in `tracklists/rage-against-authority.csv`.

- Added Sacred Reich (after Slayer), Run the Jewels feat. Zack de la Rocha (after SOAD) and Stray From The Path (late in the hardcore build).
- RTJ sits four tracks after Guerrilla Radio so Zack's voice doesn't come back too fast. It's also the hinge into the industrial block.
- Fuck Authority is the one skate punk track left, parked right after Suicidal where it makes sense. Also on Grip Tape & Bruises, that's fine for this one.
- Rise Above is the next cut if the punk stretch starts to feel soft.
