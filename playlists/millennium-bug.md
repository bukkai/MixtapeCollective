## Rework (Nov 2026)

Was Best of 00s Nu-metal. Repurposed instead of replaced, same as Two Minutes Hate and Smells Like Grunge: same Deezer playlist, same smartlink.

The rule: originally released 1998 to 2001. The peak of the genre, right when everyone was stocking up on canned food for Y2K. The old title said 00s but half the list was already from 98 and 99, so the rule just makes it honest.

- 23 of the 28 old tracks fit the rule and kept their spot. The five new ones filled the gaps left by the cuts.
- Arc: the big singles (1 to 6), the TRL middle (7 to 15), deeper cuts and second-tier bands (16 to 22), Change and Giving In as the moody stretch, Halo and Between Angels and Insects building up, Left Behind as the peak, landing on Toxicity.
- Two tracks each for Korn, Linkin Park, Papa Roach, Slipknot, Limp Bizkit, System of a Down, Deftones and P.O.D., always far apart.
- Back to School sits at 11 and Guano Apes at 14 so Youth of the Nation, Back to School and Nookie don't land as three rap verses in a row.
- Soulfly right after Ill Niño on purpose: the tribal, Latin side of the genre in one go.
- Godsmack routed out instead of swapped: closer to alt metal and post-grunge than nu-metal, and I Stand Alone is 2002 anyway.
- Lost Otep (everything on Deezer is 2002 or later), so Guano Apes took Powerman 5000's spot to keep Kittie from being the only woman singing on here. No Speech, not Open Your Eyes: that one's 1997.
- Deezer versions: Back to School from White Pony (the 2000 reissue that added it), Left Behind from Iowa (10th Anniversary Edition), studio not the London 2002 live take, Adema from the self-titled album (not the radio edit on Insomniac's Dream), No Speech from Don't Give Me Names.

## Cut

- Loco (Coal Chamber), My Own Summer (Deftones): 1997, too early
- I Stand Alone (Godsmack): 2002, too late
- Duality (Slipknot): 2004, too late
- Confrontation (Otep): 2007, way too late
- When Worlds Collide (Powerman 5000): fits the rule, made room for Guano Apes
- Charlie Big Potato (Skunk Anansie): 1999 but alt rock, adjacent but wrong

## Ideas

- Digital Bath (Deftones) almost got the spot, a good swap if Back to School ever feels too cheesy.
- People = Shit (Slipknot) is the harder option for the peak if Left Behind feels too polite there.
- Spit It Out (Slipknot, 1999), Again & Again (Taproot, 2000) and Denial (Sevendust, 1999) all fit the rule and are on Deezer.
