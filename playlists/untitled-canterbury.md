## Notes

- Came out of a r/progrockmusic thread on the most complex prog bands (Nov 9, 2026). Hatfield, National Health and Gong (via Daevid Allen) were named there, the rest is the obvious scene around them.
- Angle: the light side of complex. Warm organs, jazzy chords, silly titles, odd meters that still smile. The counterweight to Celestial Chaos, which is the dark side.
- Supersister as the Dutch detour, gives the list something of its own.
- Picchio dal Pozzo: Italian prog but strongly Canterbury-flavoured, second album named in the thread. Borderline, try it.
- Color is deep-indigo for now (prog family). Dark-purple is free if this should look different from the other prog lists.
