## Rework (Nov 2026)

Was Best of 90s Grunge. Repurposed instead of replaced, same as Two Minutes Hate: same Deezer playlist, same smartlink.

The rule: the Northwest scene and the bands in its direct orbit, mid 80s up to spring 1994. No post-grunge, no 90s alt rock that just happens to have distortion. Big names get the entry, the rest is the dirtier side the "Best of" version only touched.

- Arc: big names (1 to 5), the scene opening up (6 to 11), roots and Sub Pop sludge from Green River to Melvins (12 to 18), Negative Creep into Hooch as the peak, Rooster as the heavy comedown, landing on Chloe Dancer/Crown of Thorns.
- The ending is the lineage: Green River, Mother Love Bone and Temple of the Dog are all in here, which is why Pearl Jam gets one track.
- Two tracks each for Nirvana, Alice in Chains, Soundgarden, Mudhoney, Tad and Melvins, always far apart.
- Dinosaur Jr. is Massachusetts and SST, not Seattle, but Freak Scene is the fuzz this whole scene learned from.
- Deezer versions: Freak Scene from Bug, Hunted Down from Telephantasm (the 1987 recording), Swallow My Pride from Dry as a Bone/Rehab Doll, Wood Goblins from Salt Lick (Deluxe), Here Comes Sickness from the Mudhoney LP (not the live takes on the Superfuzz deluxe).

## Cut

- Plush, Interstate Love Song (Stone Temple Pilots), Machinehead (Bush), Tomorrow (Silverchair), Far Behind (Candlebox): post-grunge, the radio version of the sound
- Cherub Rock (Smashing Pumpkins): Chicago alt rock, adjacent but wrong
- Baby Got Shot (Verbena): 1997, too late
- Jeremy, Daughter (Pearl Jam): one Pearl Jam is enough here
- Come As You Are (Nirvana): swapped for Negative Creep
- Fell On Black Days (Soundgarden): 1994 single, swapped for Hunted Down
- Feel the Pain (Dinosaur Jr.): August 1994, swapped for Freak Scene
- Start With (Seaweed): 1995, swapped for Kid Candy
